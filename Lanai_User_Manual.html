1. Executive Summary & Platform Architecture

Welcome to the official operational manual for the Lanai Lifestyle Intelligence Platform. Lanai is a state-of-the-art, full-service luxury travel and lifestyle concierge workspace designed to synthesize high-touch human relationship management with cutting-edge artificial intelligence.

Whether you are a product owner operating Lanai as your flagship software product, a concierge advisor managing high-net-worth members, or a platform administrator, this document provides complete, transparent clarity on every feature, workflow, backend component, and user experience built into the platform.

1.1 Product Vision & Core Mission

Luxury concierge management relies on speed, hyper-personalization, zero error tolerance, and discreet communication. Lanai replaces fragmented tools (spreadsheets, messaging apps, manual document creators) with a unified ecosystem that handles client management, travel itineraries, multi-channel communications, supplier partnerships, billing, and AI co-pilots in a single workspace.

🛡️ High-Touch Human Centricity
AI acts strictly as an intelligence co-pilot. Every proposal, message draft, and financial invoice remains under total advisor control before final client delivery.
⚡ 24/7 Instant Client Responsiveness
An automated 24/7 AI Concierge ensures member inquiries receive warm, immediate responses at any hour without leaving clients waiting.

1.2 Dual-Portal Architecture

The platform maintains strict separation between operational management and the client experience through two distinct interfaces:

Portal Component Primary Audience Primary Capabilities & Purpose
Advisor Portal Concierge Advisors, Management, Finance, Operations Full CRM oversight, travel request pipeline, AI proposal co-pilot, morning briefings, supplier catalog, WhatsApp triage, invoicing, revenue analytics, and system administration.
Member Portal (Client Suite) High-Net-Worth Members & VIP Clients Private trip dashboard, interactive proposal review & approval, document vault, billing history, bespoke preference management, and 24/7 direct messaging.

2.2 Advisor Authentication Workflow

  1. Navigate to the Advisor Portal root URL. If unauthenticated, you will see the Lanai Sign-In screen featuring the brand gold crown mark.
  2. Click Sign In to initiate the Keycloak OIDC authentication flow.
  3. Enter your assigned corporate email address and password.
  4. Upon successful authentication, Keycloak issues a secure session token and redirects you to the Advisor Dashboard (`/`).
🔒 Password Policy & Session Expiry
Advisor sessions are protected by server-side Keycloak policies. Passwords must be at least 12 characters. Inactive sessions automatically expire after 8 hours of inactivity.

3. Advisor Portal: Module & Feature Guide

This section details every page and tool available in the Advisor Portal, outlining how advisors and management should utilize them in daily operations.

3.1 Executive Dashboard (`/`)

The primary control panel providing real-time operational metrics fetched live from Twenty CRM and the postgres database:

  • Total CRM Clients: Overall contact directory count.
  • Active Travel Requests: The live pipeline of ongoing client trip inquiries.
  • Active Tiered Members: Count of active Platinum, Gold, and Silver members.
  • Pipeline Value ($): Calculated total gross value of active trip opportunities.
  • Quick Actions Bar: One-click buttons to create new clients, log travel inquiries, generate AI proposals, or inspect urgent messaging alerts.

3.2 AI Morning Briefing (`/briefing`)

Designed to be an advisor's first stop every workday morning. The AI service scans the advisor's client portfolio and generates a structured daily digest:

  • Priority Follow-Ups: Highlighted client inquiries requiring immediate attention.
  • Upcoming Travel & Departures: Clients travelling within the next 48 to 72 hours requiring pre-departure verification.
  • Special Dates & Celebrations: Upcoming member birthdays or anniversaries for gift dispatch.
  • AI Recommendation Summary: Suggested daily outreach tasks to drive client retention.

3.3 Client CRM Directory (`/clients`)

The centralized database of all relationship records. Lanai utilizes a 5-Layer Profile System for every client:

  1. Basic Contact Information (Name, email, phone, corporate affiliation, social handles).
  2. Travel & Lifestyle Preferences (Cabin class, preferred hotel brands, seating, dietary restrictions).
  3. Family & Network Graph (Spouse, children, executive assistants, companion profiles).
  4. Financial & Tier Status (Lifetime spend, credit status, membership tier).
  5. Interaction History (Complete log of past trips, requests, messages, notes, and feedback scores).

Workflow 1: New Inquiry Intake & Onboarding
  • 1
    Inquiry Ingestion: A new lead arrives via website form, email, or WhatsApp message.

  • 4
    Portal Onboarding: Advisor clicks "Send Portal Invite". The client sets their secure PIN and accesses the Member Portal.

5.2 AI Proposal Generation & Booking Lifecycle

Workflow 2: End-to-End Travel Request & Proposal Delivery
  • 1
    Log Travel Request: Advisor logs a request in /travel-requests (e.g. "10-Day Amalfi Coast Superyacht Charter & Villa Stay for 6 Guests, $120k Budget").
  • 2
    Run AI Proposal Co-Pilot: Advisor opens /proposals, selects the request, and clicks "Generate Proposal". The AI generates a full day-by-day itinerary and investment breakdown in ~90 seconds.
  • 3
    Advisor Customization: Advisor reviews the draft, adjusts specific villa choices, and attaches supplier commission rates.
  • 4
    Client Portal Publishing: Advisor clicks "Publish to Client Portal". The member receives an automated notification link.
  • 5
    Member Approval & Invoicing: Member approves the proposal in /client/proposals/:id. The status in /travel-requests updates to Booked and a draft invoice is generated.

5.3 Multi-Channel Messaging & 24/7 AI Auto-Reply

Workflow 3: Inbound Messaging & AI Triage Execution
  • 1
    Inbound Message: Member sends a WhatsApp message: "Can we change our flight departure from 10am to 2pm tomorrow?"
  • 2
    AI Triage Engine: Categorizes intent as Flight Modification, urgency as High / Urgent, and flags the advisor in the Unified Inbox (`/inbox`).

    Draft Reply Generation: The AI drafts a professional response checking airline fare rules and availability.
  • 4
    Advisor Dispatch: Advisor reviews the suggested draft, confirms availability with the aviation desk, clicks send, and updates the trip timeline.

6. AI Governance, Security & Best Practices

The Lanai platform enforces strict AI governance rules to protect luxury brand reputation and client data privacy:

🛡️ Core AI Principles & Safeguards
  1. Human-in-the-Loop Supremacy: Artificial intelligence NEVER sends external client proposals, emails, or booking confirmations without explicit human advisor approval.
  2. No Factual Fabrication (Anti-Hallucination): The AI is strictly instructed to label unverified details as "Assumptions — Pending Supplier Confirmation". It will never fabricate confirmed flight numbers or live hotel rates.
  3. Server-Side Data Privacy: Client financial data and preferences are stored securely server-side in PostgreSQL. Raw database access is never exposed to browser sessions.
  4. Edge WAF Protection: The OpenAppSec ML WAF automatically detects and neutralizes SQL injections, XSS attacks, and automated bot scraping attempts.

7. Troubleshooting, FAQs & Support Escalation

7.1 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: I received a "Too Many Requests" error when generating proposals. What should I do?
A: This is an automated rate-limiting safeguard built into the APISIX API Gateway to protect server capacity. Wait 2 minutes and retry. Rate limits in production are adjusted for high-volume advisor usage.

Q: How long does AI proposal generation take?
A: Complex multi-destination itineraries typically take 60 to 120 seconds as the AI microservice cross-references client preferences, supplier catalogs, and itinerary logic.

Q: Can members see internal advisor notes or commission rates?
A: Absolutely not. The Member Portal is completely isolated. Clients only see published proposals, official invoices, uploaded documents, and direct chat messages.

Q: A client forgot their Member Portal PIN. How do I reset it?
A: Navigate to Member Portal Admin (`/member-management`), find the client's row, and click "Resend PIN Reset Invitation". The client will receive an email link to set a new PIN.

Q: Where do WhatsApp client messages land?
A: All WhatsApp interactions appear simultaneously in the WhatsApp Hub (`/whatsapp`) and the Unified Inbox (`/inbox`) with pre-triaged AI draft replies ready for review.
