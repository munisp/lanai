# Weekly Update for Ms Bolanle (template)

**How to use:** every Tuesday morning, fill this in from `PILOT_PROGRESS_LOG.md` and the live data, then send to Ms Bolanle by WhatsApp or email (her preferred channels, UR-G5). Keep it short: she should be able to read it in two minutes. Never let a Tuesday pass silently, even if the update is "no changes this week".

**Mode A** is for the build weeks (before the pilot starts). **Mode B** is for the pilot weeks (her G3 numbers). Delete the mode you are not using before sending.

## Rules for whoever sends this

1. Numbers come from the live system (SR-901 weekly summary), never from memory. If the live numbers are unavailable, say so and give them the next day rather than estimating.
2. Response times measure from client message to her sent reply (the SLA she set: urgent 15 to 30 minutes, ordinary 2 to 4 hours). Show breaches as their own line if they occurred.
3. "Chased manually" means anything she personally had to follow up on with a supplier or client beyond the system's reminders. Ask her for this line during the weekly call if it is not in the data.
4. Every decision she makes in reply (a CR approval, a scope change) is logged in `PILOT_SYSTEM_REQUIREMENTS.md` section 11 the same day, with the date.
5. Keep the tone warm and short. This update exists so she never has to ask "where are we?".
