# Lanai Lifestyle — Platform User Manual

**Version 1.0**
*Welcome to the Lanai Lifestyle intelligence platform — your complete luxury travel concierge, in one place.*

## 1. Welcome to Lanai

Lanai Lifestyle is a full-service luxury travel concierge. This platform gives you and your dedicated advisor everything you need in a single, secure workspace:

- **Live client and trip management** — every client, request, proposal and booking in one place.
- **AI-powered assistance** — proposals, client intelligence, daily briefings and message drafting, generated for you.
- **Seamless messaging** — WhatsApp and a unified inbox so nothing falls through the cracks.
- **A private client portal** — members can view their trips, documents and proposals anytime.

There are **two areas** of the platform:

| Area | Who uses it | Purpose |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Advisor Portal** | Lanai advisors & management | Run the business: clients, requests, proposals, AI, invoicing |
| **Client Portal** | Members | View your trips, documents, proposals and messages |

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Accessing the platform
Open your browser and go to the address your advisor provides (e.g. `https://lanai.newfire.app`).

### 2.2 Advisor login
1. Click **Sign in**.
2. You'll be taken to the secure sign-in page.
3. Enter your **email** and **password**.
4. You're in — you'll land on your **Dashboard**.

> **Tip:** If you forget your password, use the "forgot password" link on the sign-in page.

### 2.3 Member (client) login
1. Go to the **Client Portal** link (usually `.../client`).
2. Enter your **email** and your **4–12 digit PIN**.
3. You'll land on your personal **Trip Dashboard**.

> **First time?** You'll receive an invitation email with a link to set your PIN during onboarding.

## 3. The Advisor Portal

The Advisor Portal is the command centre for the Lanai team. The left-hand menu lets you move between areas.

### 3.1 Dashboard (`/`)
Your at-a-glance overview. It shows live, real-time numbers pulled from the CRM:
- **Total clients**
- **Open travel requests** (the active pipeline)
- **Active members**
- **Pipeline value** (live opportunities)

Use this as your daily starting point to see what needs attention.

### 3.2 Clients (`/clients`)
A searchable list of **every CRM contact**. You can:
- Search by name, company or email.
- See each client's details and contact information.
- Jump into a client's profile to see their history.

### 3.3 Members (`/members`)
Your **active membership base** (tiered: Platinum, Gold, Silver). For each member you can see:
- Their tier and status.
- Their spend / opportunities.
- Their preferences (from their profile).

### 3.4 Travel Requests (`/travel-requests`)
The **pipeline** of every client request, from "new" to "completed". For each request you can see:
- Destination, dates, number of travellers.
- Budget and special requirements.
- Status: *New → In Progress → Proposal Sent → Booked → Completed*.

This is where you manage each journey from first enquiry to confirmed booking.

### 3.5 Proposal Engine (`/proposals`) — AI Co-Pilot
The **AI proposal generator**. Instead of writing proposals from scratch:
1. Enter the client's details (name, destination, dates, travellers, budget, preferences).
2. Click **Generate**.
3. The AI drafts a complete, client-ready proposal — accommodation, day-by-day itinerary, experiences, estimated investment and next steps.
4. Review, refine and send.

> **Note:** AI generation can take a minute or two. It uses the client's facts you provide and clearly labels any assumptions — it never fabricates confirmed availability or prices.

### 3.6 AI Intelligence (`/intelligence`)
A per-member **client intelligence** view. Select a member and the AI analyses their profile to surface:
- A summary of their travel history and preferences.
- Opportunities (open requests, proposals).
- Risks and gaps (e.g. missing data to collect).

### 3.7 Morning Briefing (`/briefing`)
Generate a **daily briefing** for an advisor — a digest of the day's clients, follow-ups and priorities, so you start the day knowing what matters.

### 3.8 WhatsApp (`/whatsapp`)
The **WhatsApp AI bridge**. When a client messages you on WhatsApp, the platform:
1. Recognises the contact (and creates/updates them in the CRM).
2. Runs **AI triage** — intent, urgency, sentiment and a suggested action.
3. Drafts a **reply** for you to review and send.
4. Logs a **note** and **task** against the client automatically.

You can also generate an AI draft reply to any message right here.

### 3.9 Inbox / Chatwoot (`/inbox`)
Your **unified messaging inbox** (powered by Chatwoot). All client conversations — WhatsApp, email and portal messages — in one place. You can:
- See all conversations and who's assigned.
- Reply directly.
- Use the **AI draft** button to get a suggested reply, then edit and send.

**24/7 AI auto-reply (concierge assistant).** When a member sends a message through the portal, the platform **automatically replies on your behalf around the clock** — so no message ever goes unanswered, even outside working hours. The AI triages the message (intent, urgency, sentiment) and sends a personalised reply immediately. If the AI is unavailable, a warm acknowledgment is sent instead and the conversation is flagged for you to follow up. Your manual replies always take priority and the system never replies to its own messages.

### 3.10 Suppliers (`/suppliers`)
Your **vetted supplier network** — hotels, villas, yachts, jets, transfers and experiences. Each supplier shows category, location, rating, preferred status and contact details.

### 3.11 Invoicing (`/invoicing`)
Create and manage **client invoices** and **supplier commission invoices**. Track status (draft, sent, paid, overdue) and due dates.

### 3.12 Celebrations (`/member/:id/celebrations`)
Never miss a special date. Track members' **birthdays, anniversaries, honeymoons and more**, with reminders and gift suggestions.

### 3.13 NPS & Feedback (`/nps`)
See **member satisfaction** scores and feedback, so you can follow up and keep delighting your clients.

### 3.14 Analytics (`/analytics`)
**Revenue analytics** — see income, commissions and pipeline value at a glance.

## 4. The Client Portal (for members)

The Client Portal is your private, secure space to manage your travel with Lanai.

### 4.1 Login (`/client`)
Enter your **email** and **PIN** to sign in.

### 4.2 Trip Dashboard (`/client/dashboard`)
Your personal overview showing:
- Your **active and upcoming trips**.
- The status of each trip.
- Quick access to your documents and proposals.

### 4.3 Proposals (`/client/proposals/:id`)
View the **proposals** your advisor has prepared for you — accommodation, itinerary, experiences and investment — all in a beautiful, client-ready format. You can review and respond directly.

### 4.4 Documents
Access your **travel documents** — itineraries, confirmations, vouchers and more — in one secure place.

### 4.5 Billing (`/client/billing`)
View your **invoices and payment status** for your trips.

### 4.6 Profile (`/client/profile`)
Manage your **personal preferences** — favourite destinations, cabin class, room type, dietary needs and communication preferences — so your advisor can tailor everything to you.

### 4.7 Messages
Chat securely with your advisor through the portal. **You'll get an instant reply around the clock** — the platform's AI concierge acknowledges your message right away and routes it to your advisor, so you're never left waiting.

## 6. AI Services — What to Expect

The platform uses a **local AI assistant** to help you work faster. It's important to understand:

- **AI is a helper, not a replacement.** It drafts proposals, briefings and replies that you should **review and personalise** before sending.
- **It uses the facts you provide.** The more accurate the client details, the better the output.
- **It labels assumptions.** AI never claims availability, prices or supplier confirmations unless you supply them.
- **Generation takes time.** Complex proposals can take a minute or two.

## 7. Security & Privacy

- All access is **password/PIN protected** and sessions are secure.
- **CRM data** is protected and never exposed to the browser directly — the platform handles it securely server-side.
- Your **client data** is only visible to authorised advisors.
- We recommend using a **strong, unique password** and logging out on shared devices.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I got "Too many requests" — what do I do?**
A: This is a temporary safeguard. Wait a few minutes and try again. (In the live environment, limits are tuned for normal use.)

**Q: How long does an AI proposal take?**
A: Usually 1–2 minutes. The platform shows you when it's ready.

**Q: Can a client see my internal notes?**
A: No. The client portal only shows what you choose to share (proposals, documents, trips, billing).

**Q: Where do WhatsApp messages appear?**
A: In the **Inbox** and the **WhatsApp** page, with an AI-drafted reply ready for you.

**Q: Will members always get a reply?**
A: Yes. The 24/7 AI concierge replies to every member message automatically — with a personalised AI reply when available, or a warm acknowledgment otherwise — so no message goes unanswered, even outside working hours.

**Q: I can't log in to the client portal.**
A: Check your email and PIN. If you've forgotten your PIN, contact your advisor to resend an invitation.
