Stretch items (post-pilot, inspired by UltraNetwork)

These are the features UltraNetwork has that Lanai does not yet. They are not blocking the pilot, but they define the roadmap after launch. Ordered by impact for Bolanle.

S1

Interactive and collaborative proposals

UltraNetwork's client gives "instant feedback" on the proposal in-platform. Lanai's member portal only approve or reject. Upgrade the proposal view to let the member comment on specific sections, request changes, and accept line by line.

S2

Import supplier PDFs into proposals

UltraNetwork uploads supplier PDFs and converts them into collaborative proposals. The rebrander already extracts from email; extend it to accept PDF uploads and produce a proposal from the extracted data.

S3

Business scorecard

Pipeline value, conversion rate, and repeat clients at a glance. Lanai has revenue analytics but not a conversion or repeat-client scorecard. Add a dashboard tile that shows these three metrics front and center.

S4

Live Virtuoso or Little Emperors rate API

Integrate a live rate source so rates refresh instead of seeded baselines. Fall back to stored baselines if the API is down.

S5

Voice-first concierge assistant

UltraNetwork has a mobile voice assistant. Add voice input for the member portal (dictate a request, the AI transcribes and creates the travel request). This is the differentiator for luxury.

S6

Digital supplier agreements and platform payments

One-click supplier agreements through the platform, and secure payments direct to suppliers. Lanai tracks commission but does not contract or pay through the platform yet.

  • Enable pgcrypto; encrypt passport, emergency contact, family-office, dietary, visa, and NDA columns with a KMS-held key (AES-256-GCM or pgp_sym_encrypt).

How to use this plan

Each day has one to three concrete tasks and a clear "done when" so you know when to stop. The phases are ordered: stabilize what you have, connect the missing pieces, harden for real data, then prove it with real users. If a day runs over, push the next day rather than skipping the security phase, because the pilot holds real client PII by Day 24. I can pair with you on any day; the ones that most benefit from a second pass are Day 1 (the seed), Day 13 (the lifecycle wiring), and the whole security phase (15 to 21), which maps directly to the security report's findings.
